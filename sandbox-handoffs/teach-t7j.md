## What the fix actually is

`_coverage_fraction` used to be effectively `match.score / len(node_words)`.
`match.score` on the semantic (WordNet) tier counts a node word as present
if the text contains it, its lemma, OR a synonym of it -- so a node's
*coverage* number was measured on a different, looser vocabulary than the
text actually contains, and that inflation lands unevenly across
candidates, including whichever candidate is the tier's own winner (not
just rivals, which is what made teach-i35's fix insufficient by itself).

The fix (module docstring "COVERAGE IS MEASURED ON EXACT WORDS ONLY, EVEN
IN THE SEMANTIC TIER", teach/concept_recovery.py:108) recomputes coverage
from `node_words & text_words` -- `text_words` being the lesson's own
literal, unexpanded vocabulary (`_words(text)`, computed once in
`recover_taught_concept` and threaded through to both the raw and semantic
`_resolve_candidates` calls) -- regardless of which tier's `score` is being
checked. This decouples "how much of this node's own vocabulary is really
in the text" from "how generously this tier's matching relation credited
this node," which is the actual property the coverage tiebreak needs.
